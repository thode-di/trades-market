The salary figures have two slightly different snapshots, 2024 from elec.training and 2025 from ElectricianCourses4U. Both trace back to ONS ASHE, just different release years.

Elec.training's 2024 figures as the source is more explicit about its ONS derivation so that will be used in this project.

Day rates reflect what a self-employed electrician charges a customer, which is different from the employed salary figures. They are not directly comparable.

The gap between employed salary and self-employed day rate is itself an interesting data point since the majority of the tradesmen who would work for a platform would most likely be self-employed persons partnering up with the platform rather than being employed by the platform.
